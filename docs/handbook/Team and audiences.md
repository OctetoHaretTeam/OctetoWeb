# Team and audiences

**OctetoHaret**, FIRST Tech Challenge team **#25474**, IPLT „Spiru Haret",
Chișinău, Republic of Moldova. Founded January 2024 by eight students; now
~13 members plus volunteers, mentored by the school's computer science
teacher.

Organised into a **technical** and a **non-technical** group. That split is
structural to the whole site, not just an org chart — it drives
[[Site structure]] and the two treatments in [[Design system]].

## Awards

- Connect Award, FTC Moldova Nationals **2025** (INTO THE DEEP), 5 April 2025
- Connect Award, FTC Moldova Nationals **2026** (DECODE), 21 February 2026

Two seasons, the same award. **Outreach and connection is the team's public
identity** — which is why the [[QR system]] matters as evidence, and why
duplicating or losing an award record is treated as a serious bug.

## The name

*Octeto* = octet = 8 bits = one byte = the eight founders. The mascot is an
octopus: eight arms, eight bits.

## Audiences, in priority order

1. **Judges** scanning a QR code mid-competition on bad venue wifi. Need
   speed, awards, performance evidence, portfolio — **in English**.
2. **Sponsors** — Moldovan companies and diaspora. Need credibility, reach,
   contact.
3. **Peer teams and students.**
4. **Local press and parents.**

> [!important] Audience 1 explains most of the hard constraints
> The 3G performance budget, the QR resolver, the language switcher that works
> without JavaScript, and `/team/$slug` being the most important page — all of
> it follows from a judge with a phone at a venue.

## Constraints that follow

- **Design at 390px first.** Primary traffic is QR scans from printed shirts
  and banners.
- **Members are 14–18.** Consent defaults to off — see
  [[Why consent is applied on the server]].
- **Bilingual RO/EN** from the start. Retrofitting is the single most
  expensive mistake available here.
- **FIRST trademarks** belong to FIRST. Reference them in text; do not build
  the site's identity from them; never imply endorsement.
